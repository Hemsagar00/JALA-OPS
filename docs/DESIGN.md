# JALA-OPS — Design System

## Direction
Premium, official, government-tech, operational and field-friendly.

Use the Sri Sathya Sai District logo faithfully. Do not redesign it.

Avoid generic SaaS styling, gaming/neon UI, excessive glassmorphism, tiny labels, overcrowded cards and decorative visuals that compete with operational data.

## Colors
- Primary Navy `#0A2B66`
- Deep Navy `#071D45`
- District Orange `#F58220`
- Water Blue `#168BFF`
- Light Blue `#EAF4FF`
- Surface `#FFFFFF`
- Background `#F5F8FC`
- Text `#10294F`
- Muted `#718096`
- Success `#18A558`
- Warning `#F4A300`
- Critical `#D9343B`
- Offline `#7B8794`

Always use **icon + text + color** for operational status.

## Mobile
Android-first, 360–430px, large tap targets, one-hand use, primary actions visible immediately, normal forms under 60 seconds.

Bottom tabs: Home, Readings, Breakdowns, Tasks, Profile.

### Home priority
Station + shift + sync → Pump status → Start/Stop/Reading/Breakdown → Maintenance/Attendance → Today summary.

### Reading priority
Pump, Flow, Inlet Pressure, Outlet Pressure, Tank Level, Energy first. Remarks, GPS and Photo secondary.

## Web
Collector view is exception-driven, not an engineering register.

Executive dashboard order:
1. Water Today
2. Pump Status
3. Critical Alerts
4. Network Status
5. Water Loss / Deficit
6. Breakdown Summary
7. Data Compliance

## Design review
Use Apple HIG-based review for accessibility, form design, navigation, typography, spacing, alerts and feedback. Preserve JALA-OPS branding; do not copy Apple visual branding.
